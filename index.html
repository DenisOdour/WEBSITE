About Us

Grassroots Action Against Drug Abuse in Mathare

Mathare Community Anti-Drugs Organization (MCADO) is a grassroots-based, non-profit, non-political community-based organization established in 2018. Founded by concerned community leaders trained at the Kenya Government School through a partnership with CADCA (Community Anti-Drug Coalitions of America).

We provide a safe resource centre where young people learn, grow, and find purpose — keeping them away from drugs while also supporting people living with disabilities.

What We Do

Our Programs

Three flagship programs targeting the root causes of drug abuse and community vulnerability.

01
🗣️

Community Dialogues for Drug Abuse Prevention

Target: Youth, elders, boda boda riders, teachers, faith groups

Goal: Strengthen communal bonds and address underlying social issues

Inclusive spaces for cross-generational dialogue on drug use, unemployment, violence, and moral erosion.

  • Community forums led by trained facilitators
  • Inter-generational storytelling sessions
  • Peer support and recovery groups
  • Door-to-door outreach in high-risk areas
  • Religious and cultural institution partnerships
02
📚

School Clubs for Drug Awareness & Life Skills

Target: School-going children and adolescents

Goal: Prevent early drug initiation, foster life skills and leadership

Student-run clubs delivering vital education on drug prevention and mental wellness.

  • Drug abuse and mental health awareness sessions
  • Leadership training and mentorship
  • Creative arts: poetry, skits, painting, music
  • Conflict resolution workshops
  • Inter-school debates and talent showcases
03
👩‍💼

Economic Empowerment for Young Mothers

Target: Young mothers in informal settlements

Goal: Break cycles of poverty through skills and green entrepreneurship

Vocational training alongside emotional and financial empowerment for young mothers.

  • Skills training: baking, tailoring, leatherwork
  • Financial literacy and micro-grants
  • Therapeutic support for trauma and GBV
  • Community fairs and digital sales platforms
